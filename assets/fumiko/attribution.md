# fumiko.png

"Fumiko Complete Charset" by Sylvius Fischer (skoam)
https://opengameart.org/content/fumiko-complete-charset

License: CC0 (public domain)
Attribution not required; credited here as "created by sylvius fischer"

fumiko-blonde.png, fumiko-raven.png, fumiko-platinum.png are local hair-colour
palette shifts of the same indexed art (same index layout, recoloured
entries only). Their palettes are folded into fumiko.sprite.json as rows
1-3 of the "character" tileset (0 = red/original, 1 = blonde, 2 = raven,
3 = platinum); the three source PNGs are otherwise redundant with fumiko.png.
